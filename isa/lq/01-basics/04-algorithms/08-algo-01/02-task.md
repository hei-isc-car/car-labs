# Fibonacci Without Loops

## Tasks

1. Initialize registers for F(0) and F(1).
2. Compute F(2) to F(9) with add instructions only.
3. Keep each result in a dedicated register as shown by starter comments.
4. Do not use branches or jumps for repetition. Stop after 10 numbers.
